# Public Market POS — Buyer Manual

This guide covers everything you can do as a buyer: browsing stalls, placing orders, messaging
sellers, and checking your order history.

> Note: Screens shown in this guide are placeholders. Replace them with real screenshots before
> distributing this manual.

---

# 1. Getting Started

## 1.1 Creating a Buyer Account

Browsing and ordering currently require a Public Market POS account — there is no guest checkout.

1. On the sign-in screen, tap **Create Account**.
2. Choose **Buyer** from the two role cards.
3. Fill in your details:
   - **Full Name**
   - **Email**
   - **Password** (at least 8 characters)
   - **Phone**
4. Tap **Create Account**.

![Screenshot: buyer registration form](placeholder.png)

You'll be signed in automatically and taken to **Discover**, the buyer home screen.

## 1.2 Logging In

1. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
2. Tap **Sign In**.

> Note: One thing you *can* do without an account is view a receipt someone shares with you — a
> shared receipt link or scanned QR code opens directly, no login required.

# 2. Browsing a Seller's Stall

## 2.1 Discover Feed

1. Open **Discover** from the bottom navigation.
2. Use the search bar to search **sellers or products** by name.
3. Tap a **category chip** (e.g. Pork, Produce) to filter the feed, if categories are available.
4. Tap any product card to see its details.

![Screenshot: discover feed with category chips](placeholder.png)

## 2.2 Search by Photo

If you're not sure what something is called:

1. Tap the **camera icon** next to the search bar.
2. Tap **Tap to choose a photo** and pick or take a photo.
3. Type a short description of what it is (e.g. "pork belly").
4. Tap **Search**.

> Note: This matches your typed description against listings — the photo helps confirm what
> you're looking for, but results are currently based on the text you enter, not automatic
> visual recognition of the photo itself.

## 2.3 Viewing a Seller's Full Stall

1. On a product's detail page, tap the **Sold by** row.
2. You'll see the seller's business name, type (Retail / Wholesale / Wholesale & Retail),
   address, description, and their full list of active products.

# 3. Placing an Order

1. Open a product from **Discover** or a seller's stall page.
2. Review the **price**, **stock status** badge, and **description**.
3. Tap **Buy** (disabled and labeled **Out of Stock** if none is available).
4. In the order sheet:
   - Adjust the **quantity** with the stepper.
   - Choose a **Payment Method** — **Cash**, **GCash**, or **Maya**.
   - Confirm the **Total** shown.
5. Tap **Buy Now**.

![Screenshot: product detail page with Buy sheet open](placeholder.png)

You'll be taken straight to your digital receipt — there's no separate "waiting for seller
confirmation" step; the order completes as soon as you confirm it (as long as stock is
available).

> Note: Payment methods are recorded for the seller's records but are not processed inside the
> app — arrange payment with the seller the same way you would for a cash-register purchase.

---

# 4. Messaging a Seller

1. From a product's detail page, tap **Message Seller**.
2. This opens (or resumes) a chat with that seller, pinned to the product you were viewing.
3. Type your message and send it.
4. Find all your conversations later under **Messages** in the bottom navigation.

> Note: New messages appear automatically within a few seconds of being sent — the app checks for
> updates periodically rather than delivering them instantly.

---

# 5. Viewing Order History

1. Open **Receipts** from the bottom navigation.
2. You'll see every past order — seller name, amount, item count, and date.
3. Tap any entry to open the full receipt, which you can also share as a link or QR code from the
   seller's copy of the same receipt.
