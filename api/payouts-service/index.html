    Payout Accounts¶

    Where a seller receives non-cash payments: their GCash, Maya, bank or other e-wallet accounts, each optionally with a QR code. Buyers see these accounts on the order page, and the seller can show the matching QR to a walk-in customer. Router: app/routers/payouts.py (prefix /seller/payout-accounts), business logic in app/services/payouts.py, model SellerPayoutAccount (app/models/payout_account.py).

    All endpoints require Authorization: Bearer <token> and require_seller. Accounts hang off the seller's seller_profiles row, and every query filters by that profile, so a seller can only see and change their own accounts.

    Rules¶

    • method reuses the sales payment_method enum minus cash: gcash, maya, bank_transfer or other. Cash is never a payout account because buyers just hand it over.
    • bankName is required for bank_transfer (the bank) and other (the provider, e.g. GoTyme, ShopeePay). The schema rejects a missing one with 422.
    • Exactly one account is the default. The first account a seller creates becomes the default automatically, and setting isDefault: true on another account clears it everywhere else.
    • Lists come back default first, then oldest first.

    PayoutAccountRead: id, method, accountName, accountNumber, bankName, qrUrl, isDefault.

    GET /seller/payout-accounts¶

    List the caller's accounts. Response: list[PayoutAccountRead].

    POST /seller/payout-accounts¶

    Request (PayoutAccountCreate): { method, accountName, accountNumber, bankName?, isDefault? } (accountName and accountNumber are trimmed and must not be empty).

    Response: PayoutAccountRead (201).

    PATCH /seller/payout-accounts/{accountId}¶

    Partial update (PayoutAccountUpdate, all fields optional). Errors: 404 if the account isn't the caller's; 422 if the change leaves a bank_transfer/other account without a bankName.

    DELETE /seller/payout-accounts/{accountId}¶

    Returns 204. Errors: 404.

    POST /seller/payout-accounts/{accountId}/qr¶

    Multipart file (JPEG/PNG/WebP), saved through services/uploads.save_image and stored as qrUrl (/uploads/...). Uploading again replaces the QR. Errors: 400 (not an image), 404.

    Where the accounts show up¶

    • Buyer order page: GET /me/orders/{saleId} returns the seller's payoutAccounts, so the buyer can scan a QR or copy an account number before uploading their payment screenshot (see Sales Service).
    • Payment-method pickers: features/payouts/PaymentMethodPicker.jsx always offers Cash and greys out every other method the seller has no account for. It is used in the buyer's BuySheet and in the seller's walk-in PaymentSheet, where it shows the matching QR for the customer to scan.
    • Onboarding: after a seller registers, RegisterPage offers to add a first account (it can be skipped). Until the seller has one, the dashboard shows PayoutSetupBanner. Sellers manage accounts later under Profile → Payout accounts (PayoutAccountsSection).
